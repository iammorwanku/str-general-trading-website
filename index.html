About us

A South African multi-disciplinary company delivering practical, efficient and customer-focused solutions.

STR General Trading and Projects (Pty) Ltd is committed to providing reliable, affordable and high-quality products and services across various industries. We specialise in general trading, construction, catering, agriculture, procurement, cleaning, landscaping, transportation, logistics, property maintenance and project support services.

Our company was established with a vision to provide practical, efficient and customer-focused solutions to individuals, businesses, government departments, municipalities and private organisations.

We believe in building lasting relationships through professionalism, integrity, accountability and excellent service delivery. We approach every project with dedication, ensuring that our clients receive quality workmanship, dependable supplies and solutions tailored to their specific requirements.

We strive to contribute to economic growth, sustainable development and job creation while continuously expanding our capabilities to meet the changing needs of our clients.

Our vision

To become a trusted and recognised South African general trading and projects company, known for quality service delivery, innovation, reliability and sustainable business solutions.

Our mission

To deliver affordable, professional and high-quality products and services that meet client expectations, promote long-term partnerships and contribute positively to communities and economic development.
